Discoveries, Innovations, Evolution Meters and Economy Rebalance: 9/26/26
	- Added discoveries, found in the land around a settlement, and innovations that build on them
	- Projects are now improvements the whole settlement builds together, with later ones appearing as innovations are learned
	- Hafted Tools, Digging Sticks and Controlled Burns multiply Green Essence (x4, x6 and x8), letting settlements grow past 9 people
	- Knowledge opens with the first discovery, and Innovation after four
	- Expeditions open by finishing Scout the Surroundings at 8 people, which also widens the world map by one ring
	- Settlements store up to 300 materials, raised to 1,150 by projects, discoveries and innovations
	- Projects, discoveries and expeditions cost far more materials
	- Adaptation Points now pour straight into one chosen pressure meter, and banked points convert at a quarter of the rate
	- Meters always fit the priciest open trait, and traits cost about three times as much
	- Green Essence and Blue Essence stay within a few decades of each other up to Humanity, with Biomass well below both
	- Humanity costs 1e56 of each Essence and 5e10 peak Vitality, and a second settlement costs 1e61 of each and 1,000 materials
	- Challenges take about 20 minutes each, and Long Winter about 40
	- Finishing Ice Age melts the Ice Field back to bare ground
	- Choked Out also rewards x10 Vitality, Stagnation boosts Green Essence instead of Blue, and Sea of Light gives x1.02 per trait
	- Terrain that can't be unlocked yet no longer shows in the Terrain tab

Language Layer, Melting and Challenge Pacing: 9/25/26
	- Added the Language layer to Humanity, opened by innovating Language
	- Each settlement speaks its own language, whose words you guess from what you overhear and the simpler words they're built from
	- Learning words reaches new stages of speech, which let settlements grow into villages
	- Settlements make more from their tiles as they grow, up to x10, and rain and snow fill their springs
	- Some innovations boost the whole world, like Irrigation tripling Blue Essence
	- Water tiles evaporate after 10 minutes and Snow melts after 15, unless rain or snow tops them up
	- Wellspring's bonus shrinks smoothly instead of collapsing
	- Challenges take about 15 to 25 minutes, or 35 to 40 for ones that unlock something
	- Deadfall also resets weather and marsh upgrades, and Ice Age needs 16 icy tiles instead of 18
