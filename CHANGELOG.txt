	- The Reef sublayer is implemented, opened by the Clear Water challenge
	- It has slots where you build types of rocks with features, to meet conditions of the different fish you've unlocked
	- Fish with met conditions give bonuses

Ice Sublayer: 9/8/26
	- The Ice Field sublayer is implemented, opened by the Long Winter challenge
	- Snow falls on the sublayer, which you compact by clicking to increase the pressure
	- Compacted snow (snow -> packed snow -> ... -> ice) pays more, but each layer is a 5:1 conversion ratio
	
Marsh Sublayer: 9/7/26
	- The Marsh sublayer is implemented, opened by the Waterlogged challenge
	- Marsh sublayer up to four zones, at 1, 2, 4, and 7 marsh tiles claimed
	- Without interaction, zones end up equalizing and producing less resources
	- A healthy marsh boosts the output of the world map tiles next to it
	- A marsh with one zone pays a quarter of normal, and says it on the sublayer
